The goal is to find the most useful improvements before the final submission.

<p class="text-xs uppercase tracking-[0.18em] text-slate-500">What to say</p>

# Critique checklist

1. What is the visualisation trying to communicate?
2. Is the topic and story clear for the intended audience?
3. Are the maps and Vega-Lite charts effective?
4. Are layout, typography, colour, and annotations helping the story?
5. What should be improved first before submission?

<div class="mt-5 border border-slate-300 bg-white/60 p-4 text-sm">
Good critique is specific: name the issue, explain why it matters, then suggest a concrete fix.
</div>

# Student presentation flow

- each student gives a `two-minute` critique
- keep suggestions ranked from most important to least important
- speak to the work, not the person
- stay within time so everyone receives feedback

- feedback may cover the critique and the assignment
